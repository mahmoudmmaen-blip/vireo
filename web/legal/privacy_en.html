Vireo Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

Welcome to Vireo ("we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy is our foundational principle. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is processed and stored, and your rights to access or delete your data.

Privacy Guarantee: Vireo is designed offline-first. Your detailed meal logs, personal weight history, and health data remain strictly private and are never sold or shared with advertisers.

1. Information We Collect and Process

  • Account Information: If you sign in, we collect your email address and authentication identifier via Supabase Auth (EU Frankfurt region). Anonymous users receive an anonymous UUID on their device.
  • Nutrition & Meal Data: Photos of food and text descriptions you submit for analysis. Meal photos are processed ephemerally using Google Gemini API solely to recognize foods and estimate macronutrients. Images are not used to train public AI models.
  • Activity & Steps: Steps count, walking distance, and workout duration. When you grant Health Connect permissions, we read step counts to track your daily progress.
  • Location Data: Foreground location is requested solely when you record an outdoor GPS walking or running workout. We never access background location.
  • Purchases & Subscriptions: Handled securely by RevenueCat, Google Play, and Apple App Store. We do not store credit card details.
  • Diagnostics: Crash logs collected via Sentry (when enabled) without any personal or health metrics.

2. Where Data is Stored

Your primary data is stored locally on your device via Hive. Cloud synchronization is hosted on Supabase (EU Central region) with Row Level Security (RLS) ensuring that only your authenticated account can read or write your personal rows.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

  • Supabase: Cloud database, authentication, and secure file storage.
  • Google Gemini API: AI food photo recognition and nutrition coaching.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription entitlement and purchase verification.
  • Sentry: Crash monitoring and app performance diagnostics.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You retain full ownership of your data at all times. You can export your entire data archive (JSON and CSV) directly from the app under Profile → Privacy → Export My Data.

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app (in 3 taps: Profile → Settings → Delete Account) or via our public deletion page: Account Deletion Instructions. Deletion immediately purges all database records and storage files.

5. Contact Us

For questions or privacy inquiries, contact us at: support@vireo.app

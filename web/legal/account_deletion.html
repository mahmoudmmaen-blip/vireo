How to Delete Your Vireo Account and Associated Data

In accordance with Google Play and Apple App Store user data safety requirements, Vireo provides a direct, unconditional method to permanently delete your account and all associated personal data.

What gets deleted:
  • Your user profile, email, and authentication credentials.
  • All logged nutrition, meal photo analyses, and recipe histories.
  • All steps histories, outdoor GPS workout tracks, and fitness logs.
  • All weight entries, body composition measurements, and progress records.
  • All friendship links, challenges, and public leaderboard entries.
  • All private cloud storage files (progress photos and scans).

Note: Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

Option 1: In-App Deletion (≤ 3 Taps)

  1. Open the Vireo application on your mobile device.
  2. Navigate to the Profile tab (bottom navigation).
  3. Tap Settings → scroll to the bottom → tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the prompt by typing DELETE (or حذف in Arabic) and tap Delete Account.

Option 2: Web or Email Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can request account deletion by emailing us from your registered account email at:

Email: support@vireo.app
Subject: Account Deletion Request

Our automated systems will verify and purge your account data within 48 hours.

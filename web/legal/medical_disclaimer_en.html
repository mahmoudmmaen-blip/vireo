Medical & Wellness Disclaimer

Effective Date: October 2026

IMPORTANT NOTICE: NOT MEDICAL ADVICE

Vireo is a wellness and fitness habit app, NOT a medical device, and does NOT provide medical advice, diagnosis, or clinical treatment.

All recommendations, calorie calculations, macronutrient breakdowns, intermittent fasting protocols, workout routines, and AI coaching insights provided in Vireo are solely for general wellness, educational, and lifestyle guidance. They should never replace professional medical evaluation, diagnosis, or treatment from a licensed physician.

1. Consult a Healthcare Professional Before Starting

Always consult your physician or qualified healthcare provider before beginning any new diet, calorie-deficit regimen, fasting protocol, supplement routine, or strenuous exercise program.

Never disregard professional medical advice or delay seeking it because of information found in the Vireo application.

Never stop, adjust, or change any prescription medication without explicit consultation with your doctor.

2. Specific Health Conditions Requiring Medical Clearance

If you have any chronic medical condition, you must obtain medical approval before adopting any dietary or fasting regimen, particularly if you have any of the following 12 conditions screened within Vireo:

  • Type 1 Diabetes: High risk of diabetic ketoacidosis and severe hypoglycemia; strict medical management is required.
  • Type 2 Diabetes treated with Insulin or Oral Hypoglycemics: Medication dosages must be adjusted by your physician to prevent dangerous low blood sugar.
  • Kidney Disease: High protein diets and electrolyte shifts pose severe risks to renal function.
  • Liver Disease: Impaired metabolic capacity requires customized physician-supervised clinical nutrition.
  • History of Pancreatitis or Gallbladder Disease: High-fat or abrupt dietary shifts may trigger acute episodes.
  • Cardiovascular Disease or Heart Arrhythmias: Exercise load and electrolyte changes must be carefully monitored.
  • Familial / Inherited Hypercholesterolemia: High-saturated-fat or extreme dietary changes can elevate cardiovascular risk.
  • Blood Pressure Medication or Diuretics: Fasting and hydration changes can cause severe hypotension or electrolyte imbalance.
  • History of Gout or High Uric Acid: Rapid weight loss or fasting can trigger severe acute gout flares.
  • History of Eating Disorders: Strict calorie tracking or fasting windows can trigger unhealthy psychological cycles or relapse.
  • Frequent Fainting or Chronic Low Blood Pressure: Extended fasting can precipitate syncopal episodes and physical injury.
  • Medications Requiring Fixed Food Timing: Fasting can disrupt drug absorption and therapeutic blood concentrations.

3. Immediate Emergency Warning Signs

Stop any diet, fasting, or workout immediately and seek urgent medical care if you experience:

  • Severe dizziness, lightheadedness, or fainting
  • Chest pain, shortness of breath, or heart palpitations
  • Persistent nausea, severe abdominal pain, or recurrent vomiting
  • Abnormal, unexplainable blood glucose spikes or crashes
  • Extreme weakness or confusion

## Full description (App Store & Google Play)

**Vireo — Build strength, energy, and better habits every day**

**Vireo** is a **men's fitness app** built for real life — whether you train at home or in the gym. Plan smarter workouts, eat better, track your progress, and build the routines that support **vitality and energy** over the long run.

**What you get with Vireo:**

• **Smart workout programs** — A **home workout planner** that adapts to your equipment, goals, and schedule, with demo videos, set tracking, and built-in rest timers  
• **Practical nutrition support** — Daily meal plans, fridge-scan ingredient detection, recipe suggestions, and meal swap when you want variety  
• **Progress you can see** — Log weight, track workout adherence, and monitor weekly energy trends with clear charts  
• **Walking & daily movement** — Sync steps from Apple Health and Health Connect, with goals that grow gradually as you stay active  
• **Built for you** — Dark, premium interface; English and Arabic (RTL); guest mode to try before you sign up  

Vireo is a **vitality and energy app** for men who want structure without overwhelm — not a medical service. Always consult a healthcare professional before starting a new exercise or nutrition program.

**Download Vireo today and take the next step toward stronger habits.**

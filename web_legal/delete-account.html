Delete your Vireo account

Last updated: October 17, 2026

You can delete your account and all synced data from inside the app, or by emailing us.

In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Settings → About (or Account).
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Read the warning, then type the confirmation word.
  4. Confirm. Vireo calls the delete-account edge function, permanently deletes synced data, and signs you out.

By email

Email mahmoud.mma.en@gmail.com from the address on your account with subject Delete my Vireo account. We will process the request within 30 days.

Cycle data is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data removes it locally. After account deletion, cloud-synced logs are permanently removed.
